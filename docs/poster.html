The problem

Deadlines pile up in the same weeks.

  • Every lecturer sets deadlines alone. Nobody sees the whole cohort's calendar, so 4–5 assessments land in one week.
  • Hidden tasks. Presentations and vivas sit inside the briefs but never show on the Moodle calendar.
  • Festive weeks. Deadlines fall during Chinese New Year or Hari Raya.
  • No time for feedback. The next assignment is due before marks on the last one come back.

Who it helps

Three people, one calendar.

Students

A Monday email with this week's hours, what to start now, a "marks per hour" priority list, and an anonymous "this week is too much" button.

Lecturers

Check a new assignment against the cohort's load before publishing it. Extension requests arrive with a drafted decision.

Programme leader

Sees every crunch week on one heatmap and approves fixes in one click. Unfixable weeks are escalated with options.
