- [Monash Malaysia assessment scheduling](https://www.monash.edu.my/education-excellence/Education-Policy-and-Procedure/assessment-scheduling): timed assessments cannot fall on a weekend or a Malaysian public holiday; final exams also avoid Fridays. **Assignments may still be due on Fridays**, so this supports the festive-week rule for timed assessments only. Do not say Monash bars all deadlines on those days.

## Objections

- "Lecturers could just talk to each other." No single lecturer sees the whole cohort's calendar, so nobody can see the overload.
- "Moodle plugins already do this." They aggregate. Aggregation with no load model, no proposal and no approval trail changes nothing.
- "What stops students spamming the 'too much' button?" Final version needs APU sign-in, one response per student per week, and it only asks the programme leader to look.
- "What if the lecturer refuses?" The move is undone and the next option goes back to the programme leader (try it in the demo).
