# Evidence kit

Drafts only. Nothing here has been sent.

## Interview guide (20 minutes, programme leader or lecturer)
1. In last semester's worst week, how many assessments landed on the same cohort? What did it cost (late work, extensions, complaints, marking)?
2. Does anyone check deadlines across modules before they are published? How? How long does it take?
3. When two deadlines collide, who decides and how is it communicated?
4. Would you approve a suggested move if it came with the hours and the reason? What would you need to see?
5. Can we have one cohort's deadline list, even as a screenshot of the Moodle calendar?

Record: one number (tasks in the worst week), one quote, who said it, and permission to use it.

## Data request (short message to send yourself, edit first)
"We are building CrunchRadar for the APU hackathon. Could you share the list of assessment names, weights and due dates for one cohort this semester, and the assignment briefs if possible? We will not publish names of staff or students."

## Numbers to compute from real data
Hours in the peak week before and after the proposed moves, crunch weeks before and after, tasks found in briefs that were not on the calendar, and minutes a programme leader spends checking calendars by hand today.

## Survey (students, 30–50 responses)
How many assessments were due in your busiest week? How many hours did you spend? Did quality suffer? Would a Monday heads-up help? (Do not claim results until they exist.)
