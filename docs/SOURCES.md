# Sources

Web sources accessed 12 November 2026.

1. Malaysian Qualifications Agency. *Guidelines to Good Practices: Curriculum Design and Delivery* (2019). Under the Malaysian Qualifications Framework, 1 credit = 40 notional hours of student learning time. https://www2.mqa.gov.my/qad/v2/garispanduan/2019/GGP%20CDD/4.%20GGP%20-%20Curriculum%20Design%20and%20Delivery_BI%20-%20[FB].pdf
2. Workload Radar, Moodle Marketplace listing. Aggregates a student's deadlines across courses and flags a bucket over a threshold (vendor description). https://marketplace.moodle.com/plugins/3807
3. Brickfield Education Labs. *Assessment Bundle for Moodle*. Multi-module assessment deadlines across a programme (vendor description). https://brickfield.ie/assessment-bundle-for-moodle/
4. University of Hertfordshire. *Managing Assessments*, Learning, Teaching and Academic Quality. Staff use the assessment landscape to identify and limit assessment bunching. https://www.herts.ac.uk/ltaq/learning,-teaching-and-academic-quality/home/assessment-and-feedback/managing-assessments
5. Monash University Malaysia. *Assessment scheduling*. Timed assessments are not scheduled on weekends or Malaysian public holidays. https://www.monash.edu.my/education-excellence/Education-Policy-and-Procedure/assessment-scheduling
6. CrunchRadar student survey, 21 responses, 12 to 13 November 2026. A convenience sample shared in course group chats, not a random sample. Anonymous data, questions and method: `docs/survey/`.

The 28 hours per week limit combines [1] with two assumptions of ours (20 hours of classes and 12 hours of revision and reading), which are editable in the app.
